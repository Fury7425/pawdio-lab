A desktop app that plays a known sweep, records the return, and exports the real curve. No beautified data.

Six measurements from one signal chain.

Each one plays a generated reference and reads the return. Validated tests ship on the main pages; the rest run on the experimental page until their numbers hold up.

Latency5 presets

Output-to-input delay by FFT cross-correlation with sub-sample interpolation. Per-preset calibration removes the system baseline.

Frequency response20 Hz to 20 kHz

Log chirp sweep, stereo or mono-guided. Exports PNG and Squiglink text you can drop into a database.

ANC / transparency4 modes

A guided four-mode capture turns a marketing claim into a per-frequency attenuation curve.

Library & comparebeta

Runs persist to a local SQLite library. Overlay any two stored measurements on one axis.

Input monitorlive

Real-time dBFS meter with peak hold and clip detection, plus pink noise for placement checks.

THD, balance, crosstalk, isolationin validation

Running on the experimental page while the numbers are checked against known references.

Play a known signal, record what returns, compare.

generate sine, impulse, chirp → record the return, aligned → compare to reference

curve(f) = 20 · log10( rec(f) / ref(f) )

The Rust engine generates the signal with a fade envelope. Input and output streams run at once with a safety margin, FFT cross-correlation aligns the recording, and magnitude ratios become the curve, averaged across repeats.
