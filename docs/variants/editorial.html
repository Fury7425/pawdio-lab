01The thesis

Spec sheets round the corners. A coupler and a sweep do not. Pawdio Lab plays a known signal, records what comes back, and reports the difference as the curve your gear actually produces.

02The instrument

Validated tests live on the main pages. The rest stay on the experimental page, labelled honestly, until their numbers hold.

Six measurements, one signal chain.

Latency5 presets

Output-to-input delay by FFT cross-correlation with sub-sample interpolation. Per-preset calibration subtracts the system baseline.

Frequency response20 Hz to 20 kHz

A log chirp sweep, stereo or mono-guided, exported as PNG or Squiglink text.

ANC / transparency4 modes

A guided four-mode capture turns a noise-cancelling claim into a per-frequency attenuation curve.

Library & comparebeta

Runs persist to a local SQLite library, ready to overlay any two on a single axis.

Input monitorlive

A real-time dBFS meter with peak hold and clip detection, plus pink noise for placement checks.

THD, balance, crosstalk, isolationin validation

Running quietly on the experimental page while the numbers are checked.

04The method

Play a known signal. Record what returns. Compare.

01
Generate
A sine, impulse, or log chirp with a fade envelope, written by the Rust engine.
02
Record, aligned
Input and output streams run at once with a safety margin. FFT cross-correlation lines the recording back up.
03
Compare to reference
Magnitude ratios become the curve, averaged across repeats.

curve(f) = 20 · log₁₀( rec(f) / ref(f) )
