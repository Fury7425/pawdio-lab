Under the hood

The shortest path from sound to insight.

The workflow is direct by design: create a known reference, record what survives the path, and compare the two.

01 / GENERATE

Play a known signal

Sine, impulse, pink noise, or a logarithmic chirp with a controlled fade envelope.

02 / ALIGN

Record what returns

Native audio I/O captures the response while cross-correlation resolves the timing offset.

03 / COMPARE

Read the difference

Magnitude ratios, repeat statistics, and channel comparisons become usable measurements.
