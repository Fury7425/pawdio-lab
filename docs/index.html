How a measurement works

1

Play a known signal

The Rust engine generates a sine, impulse, or log chirp with a fade envelope and plays it through your output device.

sine, impulse, chirp
2

Record what returns

Input and output streams run simultaneously; capture covers the signal duration plus a safety margin.

duration + margin
3

Compare against the reference

FFT cross-correlation aligns the recording, magnitude ratios become the curve, and repeats are averaged with a standard deviation.

20 · log10(rec / ref)

Rust does the DSP, React draws the results. Native device I/O, no browser audio stack in the signal path.
